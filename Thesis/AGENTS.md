# Thesis writing

- Before drafting or revising thesis prose, read `Thesis/Writing-style-guide.md`
  and `Thesis/Chapter-overview.md`. Follow the guide's explanatory voice,
  terminology and evidence rules.
- Write thesis prose in English. Planning notes and discussion may use the
  user's requested language.
- `Thesis/Writing-style-analysis.md` documents the evidence from the German
  bachelor thesis. Its observed weaknesses are not features to imitate.
- Change only the sections agreed with the user. Preserve existing formatting
  unless a formatting change is authorised.
- Check model statements against the current code, literature claims against
  the actual sources, and numerical findings against saved results. Distinguish
  published findings, project assumptions and interpretations.
- Record lasting writing-style decisions in `Thesis/Writing-style-guide.md`,
  so that future work can follow them across chats.
